# Code of Conduct

Janitor is a collaborative project. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Assume good intent while addressing harm clearly.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted private contact.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when conduct harms the project or its community. They will aim to explain
enforcement decisions and apply these expectations consistently.

Report serious or private concerns to the repository owner through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Swellshinider). Do not use public issues for
private conduct reports.
